Hi, I'm Brian.

A wake-up call

In spring 2024, I was diagnosed with fatty liver disease and told I had high cholesterol and obesity based on BMI. Nothing like a medical report to remind you you're not invincible.

The unexpected shift to cycling

In summer 2024, I started cycling. I lost 40 lbs, my cholesterol improved, and I discovered something unexpected: riding a bike in Toronto is both the best workout and the most unpredictable commute I've ever had.

Everyday commuting reality

I don't own a car, so cycling isn't a hobby — it's transportation. Winter commuting in Toronto builds character, patience, and a strong appreciation for wind that always seems to be against you, no matter which direction you're going.

The red light incident

One Sunday morning, while stopped at a red light, I was hit by a driver. They told me I was "taking up too much room." I've since learned that cars have a unique ability to make you feel both invisible and in the way at the same time.

Why this site exists

I now ride with cameras to document what cyclists experience every day in Toronto. This site exists so people can share why cycling matters — or doesn't — and to make space for real stories from the road.

CAN-BIKE certification

I became CAN-BIKE certified, with instructor certification currently pending. I didn't do it for the certificate — I did it because Toronto traffic has a way of humbling even the most confident cyclist. The training helped me better understand positioning, awareness, and how small decisions can make a big difference when you're sharing the road with several tonnes of metal moving unpredictably fast.
