Features

Professional tools.
Zero complexity.

Built for videographers and media professionals who work with large libraries across multiple removable drives.

Local speech-to-text New
Transcribe your footage directly on device using whisper.cpp with Metal GPU acceleration. Timestamped segments are stored locally and searchable — click any line to jump to that moment in the video. No data leaves your machine.
Lossless clip export Standout
Set In and Out points on any video, then export the clip with zero re-encoding using FFmpeg stream copy. Your footage stays at original quality — no compression artifacts, no waiting for a transcode.
Keyword auto-marking New
Say a keyword phrase while recording — like "mark video" — and after transcription the app automatically creates a marker at that exact timestamp. Define your phrase once in Settings, mark as many moments as you need while filming.
Tabbed detail panel New
Asset details are now organised across three focused tabs — Info (metadata, tags, locations), Markers (interactive timeline + list), and Transcript. Each tab stays in context while you work through footage.
Visual marker timeline New
Markers are now displayed on a proportional timeline in the Markers tab. Point and range markers appear at their exact timestamps — click any marker to jump the video player to that position.
Full-text search
Search filenames, tags, descriptions, and transcript content from a single search bar. Results update instantly across your entire library.
Drive management
Register any external drive or folder. Online and offline status is detected automatically when you connect or disconnect drives.
Inline video playback
Preview footage directly in the app. Scrub the timeline, jump to any marker, and review clips without opening an external player.
Markers & tags
Add point and range markers with names to any asset. Tag assets for cross-drive filtering. Add descriptions and locations for richer search.
Local-first & private
SQLite database stored on your Mac. No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry. Your footage and transcripts never leave your machine.
Light & dark mode
Follows your macOS system appearance automatically. Override to light or dark in Settings.
Audio metadata
Audio codec and channel count are now extracted and displayed for every asset — useful for identifying stereo vs mono recordings and verifying audio format before export.
Source management
Improved drive registration flow with per-drive media type selection — choose to index Video, Image, Audio, or any combination independently. Preferences are saved per drive and pre-filled on re-index.
Built with

Small binary.
Fast startup.

Tauri uses the system WebView instead of bundling Chromium — the result is a native-feeling app that launches in under a second. Rust handles all performance-critical work: indexing, thumbnails, transcription, and database I/O.
